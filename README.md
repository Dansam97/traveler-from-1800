One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. It builds only from what such a traveler already knows — libraries, letters, wires, a cup of tea going cold — and carries the whole picture in a single comparison: every library on Earth joined by wires thinner than thread, so any book, letter, or picture can be copied to any house before the tea cools. The program validates the sentence against the gate's rules (one line, one sentence, ≤200 characters, UTF-8) and writes it to `entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
